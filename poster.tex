  {\large\textbf{\color{TealDark}About the symposium}}\\[4pt]
  {\footnotesize
  Living Networks, including societies, groups and brains, continuously reshape their structure, interactions, and information flow---yet their large-scale function remains remarkably invariant.\\[4pt]
  This three-day international symposium combines perspectives from complex systems, physics, data science, neuroscience, and sociology to uncover common principles of self-organization, resilience, and vulnerability to manipulation across systems and scales.\\[4pt]
  \rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}\\[3pt]
  \textbf{Organized by:} Julia Fischer, Greta Jasser, Thomas Kneib, Piklu Mallick, Viola Priesemann.
