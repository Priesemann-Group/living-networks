How to Reach the Venue (MPI-DS)

Göttingen is centrally located in Germany with direct high-speed ICE railway connections.

By Train

Book your train tickets at Deutsche Bahn (bahn.de) →

From Göttingen Train Station (Bahnhof) to MPI-DS

From the central bus station right in front of Göttingen Hauptbahnhof (ZOB / Bahnhof), take bus line 21, 22, or 23 directly to the bus stop “Faßberg” (approx. 15–20 minutes). The MPI-DS campus entrance is located directly adjacent to the bus stop.

By Plane

  • Frankfurt Airport (FRA): Direct ICE high-speed trains depart from Frankfurt Flughafen Fernbahnhof (Terminal 1) directly to Göttingen in under 2 hours without train transfers.
  • Hannover Airport (HAJ): S-Bahn to Hannover Hauptbahnhof and a 35-minute ICE ride to Göttingen (~1h total).
  • Hamburg Airport (HAM): S-Bahn to Hamburg Hauptbahnhof + ICE to Göttingen (~2.5 hours total).

Accommodation in Göttingen
