Context

Background and aims

From November 30 to December 2, 2026, the University of Göttingen will host an interdisciplinary symposium bringing together international experts in physics, data science, neuroscience, law, and social and political sciences.

With invited and contributed talks, poster sessions and ample time for discussion, we will sharpen our understanding of how self-organization mechanisms drive structure and information flow in social and neural networks. Topics include disinformation and polarization, societal resilience, network-based interventions and the ethics of manipulating living information systems.

The symposium is organized by the CIDAS Section Complexity, Information, Cognition, Society (CICS), funded by the University of Göttingen, and aims to foster future collaborative research initiatives across disciplines.

Key questions

  • How do neural and social networks maintain robust function despite continuous structural change?
  • Which self-organization principles are shared across brains, societies and technological systems?
  • How do disinformation and polarized information flow emerge, and how can they be mitigated?
  • What are ethical and legal constraints on manipulating living networks?
